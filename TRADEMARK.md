## Why this dual structure

The GPL guarantees that the source code remains free for anyone to
study, modify, and redistribute. The trademark guarantees that the
name "ZenVizor" identifies a specific, recognized product with a
known origin. Together they let the project be open source without
ceding control of its brand identity. This is the same model used by
Mozilla (Firefox), Audacity, Krita, and GIMP, among others.
